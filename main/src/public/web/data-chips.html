How it works

Rules

  1. You start with 200 Data Chips and 3 resets.
  2. Buying an app costs 1 chip.
  3. A purchase pauses the time loss for 5 weeks.
  4. After protection ends, you lose 3 chips every 2 days.
  5. At 0 chips, your balance freezes until you use a reset.
  6. A reset restores 200 chips and uses one reset.
  7. When all 3 resets are gone, 0 chips stays at 0.
